Collection Gaps and Phased Investigation Roadmap

Sandpoint / Bonner County Developer Influence Investigation

This document consolidates all known collection gaps from the investigation's working files, organizes them into actionable phases, and identifies what has been completed, what is in progress, and what remains. The goal is to maintain investigation momentum and prevent the engagement from losing focus.

Analytical Caveats and Disclaimers

All inferences in this investigation are analytical opinions based on patterns observed in public records. They are clearly denoted as inferences (not facts) wherever they appear. Inferred connections on the Investigation Board are shown as dashed lines and labeled "INFERRED" with the analytical reasoning documented.

Verified findings are supported by cited public records (Secretary of State filings, court documents, campaign finance disclosures, government meeting minutes, county assessor/recorder data, and news reporting). Each verified connection cites its source.

This investigation uses only publicly available information. No private databases, subscription services, or unauthorized access methods were used. All findings can be independently verified by any citizen or journalist using the same public sources. Nothing in this report should be construed as an allegation of criminal activity or legal wrongdoing; patterns are documented for public awareness and further inquiry.

Investigation Momentum Recommendations

Preventing Engagement Stall
  • Set a 2-week cadence. Every two weeks, produce one deliverable: an entity appendix, a campaign finance update, or a property records analysis. Cadence prevents drift.
  • Prioritize Phase 3 (SOS portal automation). Most of the open gaps are entity verification questions that direct SOS searches would close. This is the single highest-value next step.
  • Time-box HUMINT integration. Process intelligence from the Sandpoint contact within 48 hours of receipt. Context decays. Notes from meetings and conversations should be documented the same day.
  • Set calendar alerts for May 16 (Mountain Homestead refiling window) and monitor the Sandpoint planning portal monthly for Averill condo plat filings.
  • Maintain the Investigation Board. As new findings are verified, add nodes and edges to the knowledge graph. The board is both a deliverable and a working analytical tool.
  • Track a "verified" vs. "gap" ratio. Currently, approximately 65% of connections on the Investigation Board are verified with cited sources. Target 85%+ by end of Phase 4.
