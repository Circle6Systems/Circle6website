Evidence-led framing

As of the FDA CORE outbreak table (Last-Modified 2026-09-02 17:59 UTC), FDA lists 13 outbreaks with Outbreak/Event Status "Ongoing." Of those, 6 carry a specific-product public consumer advisory (Outbreak/Event Status "Ongoing See Advisory"), and 7 do not (status "Ongoing See Advice," meaning generic food-safety advice only, no named product), representing 486 named cases with no consumer-facing advisory to act on. FDA's own table states its advisory policy in these words: "A public health advisory will be issued for investigations that have resulted in specific, actionable steps for consumers to take to protect themselves.... FDA will only indicate a product category in the table and will not publicly name a specific product until there is sufficient evidence to implicate that product." That policy, not any claim about intent, is the reason the gap exists: an advisory requires an identified product, and 7 of the 13 ongoing investigations have not yet reached that evidentiary bar. This page makes no claim about motive; it reports what FDA's own status column shows.

Methodology, how we count

Ongoing means FDA's CORE outbreak table lists the investigation's Outbreak/Event Status as "Ongoing" (as opposed to a status indicating the investigation has ended). Advisory Yes/No is read directly from that same status cell: "Ongoing See Advisory" is counted Yes, a specific public consumer advisory has been published for the investigation; "Ongoing See Advice" is counted No, only generic consumer advice is offered because, per FDA's own policy note above, the implicated product has not yet been confirmed to the evidentiary standard FDA requires before naming it. Case counts are FDA's own published counts where a table figure exists; the 6 advisory outbreaks show "see advisory" in FDA's table rather than a running case count, so they are not included in the 486-case sum, and the sum covers only the 7 no-advisory outbreaks. Outbreaks whose status is not "Ongoing" (recently ended investigations) are excluded from the denominator and are not part of this page's scope. This page is primary-source only; no figure here is drawn from secondary reporting. CDC operates a parallel, separate outbreak-notice policy for identified clusters, illustrated by the Cyclospora cluster below; CDC states that clusters without an identified source and specific consumer action do not receive a CDC outbreak notice, the same evidentiary-threshold logic FDA describes for its own advisories.

2026 Cyclosporiasis Outbreak, Full Briefing

The largest of the 13 ongoing events by far: CDC's national cyclosporiasis surveillance count stands at 18,445 lab-confirmed cases, 990 hospitalizations and 2 deaths across 49 states plus the District of Columbia (CDC, as of Sept 1, 2026). The Taylor Farms de Mexico iceberg-lettuce cluster (Ref 1390 above) is one of the 6 outbreaks with a published FDA consumer advisory. The 3 Cyclospora rows in the table above with no yet-identified product (Refs 1392, 1385, 1398, together 253 cases) are separate, unattributed investigations covered in the full briefing's outbreak-count discussion.
