State Involvement Map

Per-State Case Counts and Outbreak Attribution
Product Geospatial Intelligence (GEOINT)
TLP CLEAR
Published August 4, 2026 (v22)
Lens Geographic spread
Reliability Mixed (state / CDC-feed, labeled)
Read time ~4 minutes

Color shows the magnitude of reported cases; the outlined states are the 9-state Taco Bell / Taylor Farms de Mexico lettuce cluster (Illinois, Indiana, Kansas, Kentucky, Michigan, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, West Virginia), expanded from 5 states when CDC retitled its outbreak page on Jul 24. Most green states report only sporadic, seasonal, or travel-related cases and explicitly state theirs are not linked to the lettuce outbreak. Hover or focus any state for its figure, basis, and source.

Reading this map honestly

  • Two number systems, kept separate. State health departments publish confirmed + probable totals (highest, most current). CDC's weekly feed counts lab-confirmed cases only and lags, so its per-state figures (marked with the approx sign) are systematically lower. Do not compare a bold state total against a CDC-feed figure as if equal.
  • The 9-state lettuce cluster (bold outline): Illinois, Indiana, Kansas, Kentucky, Michigan, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, West Virginia. CDC added Illinois, Kansas, Oklahoma, and Pennsylvania on Jul 24, retitling its outbreak page from 5 to 9 states. Michigan's, Kansas's, and North Carolina's larger state totals are only partly attributable to this cluster; North Carolina is a separate outbreak, not lettuce-linked.
  • Gray means no published figure. Gray states publish no 2026 cyclosporiasis case count, which is not the same as zero cases. The five currently in this status are Montana, DC, Minnesota, North Dakota, and Vermont.
  • Endemic-baseline states (Texas, Florida) carry large cyclosporiasis counts every summer, so moderate 2026 numbers there may be normal, not outbreak-driven.

Primary sources: CDC surveillance and HAN00531; state health-department releases; CDC weekly feed via the GenomicEpi aggregator for approx figures. Figures are time-sensitive and update as states report.
