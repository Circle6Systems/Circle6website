2026 US Cyclosporiasis Outbreak

Multistate · Reporting period from May 1, 2026 · Briefing v2, July 15, 2026 (data as of Jul 14)

Open-source threat-intelligence briefing on the largest domestic Cyclospora cayetanensis event on record. A parasite spread through the fresh-produce supply chain has produced 1,645 CDC-confirmed domestic cases across 34 states, with more than 5,100 under analysis (nearly 7,000 combined), now exceeding the ~4,700-case US record set in 2019. Lettuce and salad greens have emerged as a suspected vehicle and federal and state investigators are examining Taco Bell, but no food source has been confirmed and no recall issued. We assess the outbreak as a real-world stress test of degraded US biosurveillance and food-safety trace-back capacity.

Bottom Line Up Front

This is a clinically mild but epidemiologically significant outbreak. Severity is low: the illness is self-limiting, treatable with antibiotics, and not spread person-to-person. The strategic signal is elsewhere. Confirmed cases have roughly doubled since v1 to 1,645 (nearly 7,000 counting the under-analysis pool), a source is only now emerging months in, and this all coincides with the July 2025 reduction of CDC's FoodNet active surveillance from eight pathogens to two (Cyclospora among those dropped) and an approximately 25% cut to the CDC workforce. The system can still count cases; it is visibly slower to act on them.

What changed since v1 (Jul 13 → Jul 15). CDC-confirmed cases rose 843 → 1,645 across 34 states (several media maps show 32); under-analysis grew >1,500 → >5,100; hospitalizations 86 → 141; deaths remain 0. The event now exceeds the ~4,700-case 2019 US record. Most significantly, a candidate vehicle emerged: Michigan officials say early information points to lettuce or salad greens, FDA is investigating produce, and federal and state agencies are examining Taco Bell (per Washington Post reporting), which voluntarily and temporarily pulled limited ingredients at select restaurants. No product, supplier, grower, region, or restaurant has been officially confirmed.

Predictive Assessments

Predictions are time-stamped at publication and tracked for calibration. Resolution status is updated as confirming or disconfirming evidence emerges. Baseline date: July 13, 2026 (v1); updated July 15, 2026 (v2).
Prediction 1: A leafy-green or fresh-herb vehicle

If a food vehicle is ever identified, it will be a leafy-green/salad blend or a fresh herb (basil/cilantro), consistent with every resolved US Cyclospora outbreak since 2013, not an exotic new commodity.

v2 update (Jul 15): Trending toward confirmation. Michigan officials now cite lettuce or salad greens as the probable vehicle and FDA is investigating produce, consistent with this prediction, though no vehicle is officially confirmed. Confidence raised 70% to 80%.
