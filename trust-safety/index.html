In Memory

Daniel Records-Galbraith

February 10, 1984 — April 28, 2026

This guide was created in honor of Daniel, whose life was defined by a commitment to civil rights, equity, and making people feel less alone. He was the kind of person who made institutions more human.

Memorial donations may be made to Inland Oasis, the LGBTQ+ nonprofit serving the Palouse region where Daniel served as president.
